⚠️ ¿Qué pasa si pagás tarde?

  • Recargo por mora del 5% por día sobre el total a devolver, desde el primer día de atraso (sin días de gracia).
  • El recargo se acumula hasta un tope del 100% del total: en el peor caso tu deuda se duplica.
  • El préstamo pasa a estado VENCIDO.
  • Tu nivel baja según cuánto te atrases (la mora no cambia):
    • Hasta 3 días: se congela — no baja, pero ese préstamo no suma para subir.
    • 4 a 15 días: bajás un nivel — lo recuperás devolviendo a tiempo.
    • Más de 15 días, o un préstamo VENCIDO sin pagar: volvés al límite Inicial ($150.000) y reconstruís tu historial.

How it works

The more you repay on time, the more you can borrow. Each loan paid punctually moves you up one step and unlocks a higher limit.

Levels & borrowing limit

LevelYou can borrow up toHow to get there
Starter$150,000New client / no history
Level 1$250,0001 loan repaid on time (of at least $150,000)
Level 2$350,0002 on-time repayments, one of at least $250,000
Level 3$450,0003 on-time repayments, one of at least $350,000

Many small loans do not move you up: you must prove progressively larger amounts and pay them on time.

⚠️ What happens if you pay late?

  • Late surcharge of 5% per day on the total to repay, from day one of delay (no grace period).
  • The surcharge builds up to a cap of 100% of the total: worst case, your debt doubles.
  • The loan is marked OVERDUE (VENCIDO).
  • You lose your level: the ladder resets to the Starter limit ($150,000) and you must rebuild your history. A loan paid late never counts toward moving up.

✓ If you pay on time

You climb a level, unlock higher amounts and longer terms, and keep your record In good standing.
